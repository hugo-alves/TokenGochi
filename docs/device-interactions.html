What should I click?

Use this when the watch is on your wrist and you need the button pattern fast. KEYA is the yellow button. KEYB is the blue button. Touch is only a direct selector on the recording-duration settings screen.

A = back, cancel, page, yes on reset B = enter, start, send, dismiss, no on reset A + B hold = recording length

Stats

Usage and connection

Reset confirm

Be deliberate

A short confirms reset. B short cancels back to stats.

Settings

Recording duration

tap selects 10, 20, or 30 seconds, then a saved screen appears before returning home. B short cycles. A short exits.

Error

Something failed

A short or B short dismisses early. It also auto-dismisses after a short timeout.

Patterns
A pattern

Leave safely

A usually means back, cancel, or page. The only exception is reset confirm, where A means yes.

B pattern

Proceed

B usually moves forward: enter voice, start recording, send, dismiss, open reset, or cancel reset.

Touch pattern

Only direct choices

Touch is used for settings options. It is not the primary control for pet, voice, recording, or transcript.

Round Screen
10 sec 20 sec 30 sec

The corners do not matter

The watch screen is round, so usable choices stay centered inside the visible disc. In settings, tap the option rows in the middle of the screen. Do not aim for corners; they are outside the practical touch target model.

Guards
Accidental settings

A+B only opens from safe modes

Use A+B hold from pet, voice ready, or stats. It does not open while recording, sending, transcript, reset confirm, or error.

During recording

Buttons are the controls

B sends. A cancels. Touch is ignored so a palm or sleeve does not change settings or trigger a hidden action.

During transcript

Touch pages text

Tap anywhere on the transcript screen to move to the next page. B still dismisses back to the pet.

WiFi note: There is no on-watch pairing menu. The device connects on boot to the SSID compiled into firmware secrets. To always use your phone, flash firmware with your phone hotspot SSID and password, then keep that hotspot available.
